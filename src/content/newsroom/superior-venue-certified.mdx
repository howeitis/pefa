PEFA and Microsack today certified the first Superior Venues™ for the 2036 season.

Every certified venue holds the pitch at a constant 19°C under a closed roof, and features dynamic
ticket pricing, personalised in-seat advertising, AI-generated chants, haptic seating and
real-time Micro-Betting™. Complimentary financial wellness content is included with every ticket.

Certification confirms that each venue’s crowd audio is calibrated to broadcast tolerances.
