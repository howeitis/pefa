---
title: A clarification on the word "football"
date: 2035-11-12
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: The word remains subject to ongoing trademark review. Supporters may continue to use it.
---

Following questions from supporters and the media, PEFA wishes to clarify its position on the
word "football".

The word "football" is subject to ongoing trademark review. Pending the outcome of the review,
PEFA will continue to use the word under licence from itself.

Supporters may continue to use the word in private conversation. Use in chants is covered by the
Superior Venue™ chant catalogue, in which every lyric has been cleared for rights.

PEFA has no plans to charge for the word. PEFA has no plans.

**Notes to editors**

Media organisations are asked to describe the Superior League as "football" until further notice,
and to retain this notice.
