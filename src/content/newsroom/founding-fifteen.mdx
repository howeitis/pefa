---
title: The Superior League confirms its fifteen founding members
date: 2035-06-30
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: Fifteen permanent, un-relegatable seats, held in perpetuity.
---

PEFA today confirmed the fifteen founding members of the Superior League. Each holds a permanent,
un-relegatable seat, and each is guaranteed its revenue regardless of performance, form, or final
position.

Founder status is not contingent on winning, qualifying, or attending.

The remaining five places in each season will be allocated by the Synergy Draft™, an algorithmic
lottery drawn each summer from a pool of thirteen global partners and weighted by subscription
tier, market size, brand compatibility and broadcast reach.

> “Fifteen seats. Held in perpetuity. We think that has a ring to it.”
>
> — Office of the President

The founding members’ listing documents are available from [Investor Relations](/investors).

**Notes to editors**

The selection criteria for founding members have not been published. PEFA considers the list
self-explanatory.
