title: President Infamtino delivers PEFA™ launch address
date: 2035-05-21
dateline: Dublin
summary: '"We have not taken the game from anyone. We have asked them to subscribe."'
---

President Giacomo Infamtino today delivered PEFA's launch address from a data center in Dublin,
setting out the organisation's vision for a football competition free of avoidable risk.

The address was streamed to partners, investors and, where commercially appropriate, supporters.
It was delivered from the data center because, in the President's words, "that is where the game
now lives".

> "We have not taken the game from anyone. We have asked them to subscribe."
>
> — G. Infamtino, launch address

The President confirmed that PEFA's technology partners, Microsack, Clawed AI and ChatGDP, had
modelled recovery scenarios for the sport and reached a unanimous conclusion. He declined to state
the conclusion, noting that it would be "obvious in the fullness of time".

ChatGDP generated the supporter reaction to the address in advance. It was positive.
