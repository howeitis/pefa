---
title: Private Equity Football Accelerate™ incorporated in Luxembourg
date: 2035-05-14
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: A new organisation to address football’s structural defect, the possibility of a bad outcome.
---

Private Equity Football Accelerate™ (“PEFA”) was today incorporated in Luxembourg, nine days after
the dissolution of the Global Broadcast Rights Consortium.

PEFA has been formed to address the structural defect exposed by the collapse of long-format
broadcast revenue: the possibility of a bad outcome. Following the 2033 finding that highlight
packages outperformed live matches at one-fortieth the production cost, long-form rights
collapsed within eighteen months. Four of Europe’s six largest domestic competitions have since
entered administration.

PEFA’s President is Giacomo Infamtino. The organisation has five stated objectives, which will be
set out in its public filings.

> “Football does not need saving. It needs structuring.”
>
> — G. Infamtino, President

Further announcements will follow in the coming weeks.

**Notes to editors**

PEFA™ is registered in Luxembourg. The timing of its incorporation relative to the dissolution of
the Consortium is a coincidence.
