---
title: Synergy Draft™ 2036 completed
date: 2036-06-20
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: Five berths drawn from thirteen global partners. Every continent represented. Every continent paid.
---

PEFA confirms that the Synergy Draft™ for the 2036 season has been completed.

Five berths were drawn from the pool of thirteen global partners by algorithmic lottery, weighted
by subscription tier, market size, brand compatibility and broadcast reach. Applications were
assessed by the Global Inclusion Committee, which met in the spring and did not publish its
reasoning.

The five clubs drawn have been notified. The result is available to subscribers at every tier,
from the day of the draw, in the Superior League app™.

> “No region is excluded. Some are simply more included.”
>
> — O. Adeyemi-Clarke, Chair, Global Inclusion Committee

Berths are redrawn every summer, regardless of where their holders finish.
