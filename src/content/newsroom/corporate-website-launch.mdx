Private Equity Football Accelerate™ ("PEFA") today launched its corporate website, a single
destination for investors, partners and, where commercially appropriate, supporters.

The site brings together PEFA's public filings, the Official Media Kit, the Superior League
decree register and the latest statements from the Newsroom. Supporters will find answers to
frequently asked questions, including whether their club has been relegated. It has not. It was
not invited.

> "Transparency is one of our five stated objectives. We are pleased to confirm it is one of the
> two that are not redacted."
>
> — Office of the President

The website has been optimised for engagement. Time spent on each page is recorded as value
generated, although, in keeping with PEFA's privacy commitments, it is not recorded anywhere.

**Notes to editors**

PEFA™ is the owner and operator of the Superior League. The word "football" is subject to
ongoing trademark review.
