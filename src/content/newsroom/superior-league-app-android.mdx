---
title: The Superior League app™ is now available on Google Play
date: 2036-10-28
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: Supporters on Android may now manage a club in the league, subject to the league.
---

PEFA today made the Superior League app™ available to supporters on Google Play. The app is free.
PEFA regards this as a pricing decision, not a precedent.

For the first time, supporters may take charge of a club in the Superior League from a device they
already own. They will pick the eleven, set the tactics, work the transfer market and answer to the
board. The match engine will then explain why they were wrong.

Supporters should note that the app has been certified as engaging. Notifications are delivered at
moments of peak emotional availability. Supporters may switch them off, which is recorded as
feedback.

> “For years supporters told us they could run the club better. We listened, and we have made it a
> product. Early results suggest they cannot, which is reassuring for everyone.”
>
> — P. Kallend, Chief Engagement Officer

The app carries every league decree in force, including those not yet announced. Results are final
on publication and binding on the supporter.

The Superior League app™ is available now on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.howeitis.superiorleague),
and in any modern browser on [the web](https://superior-league-2036.vercel.app/).

**Notes to editors**

PEFA™ is the owner and operator of the Superior League. Downloading the app does not confer
membership, ownership or a say.
