title: PEFA™ notes supporter-trust proceedings (not material)
date: 2036-03-03

summary: PEFA does not recognise legacy supporter-trust "litigation", and does not consider it material.
---

PEFA notes reports of proceedings brought by a number of legacy supporter trusts.

PEFA does not recognise legacy supporter-trust "litigation". PEFA does not consider these
proceedings material.

PEFA respects the passion of supporters, which it has measured, and which remains within
tolerance. Supporters with concerns are invited to raise them through
[Supporter Services](/support), where every message is received, valued and answered.

> "We have read the claim. We have decided not to have read it."
>
> — A. Kvist, General Counsel

**Notes to editors**

This statement does not constitute recognition of the proceedings, including the proceedings it
refers to.
