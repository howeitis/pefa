---
title: The Superior League app™ enters open iOS testing on TestFlight
date: 2036-11-24
dateline: Luxembourg
summary: Supporters on iPhone may now join the open TestFlight programme, ahead of a full App Store listing.
---

PEFA today opened the Superior League app™ to supporters on iPhone through Apple TestFlight.
Participation is free and open to any supporter with a compatible handset and a willingness to be
measured.

Following last month’s arrival on Google Play, the iOS build brings the full Superior League
management experience to a second mobile ecosystem: thirty clubs, every league decree, transfer
negotiations, and the Clawed AI Cup. Supporters pick the eleven, set the tactics and answer to the
board; the match engine remains unpersuaded by brand loyalty.

TestFlight allows supporters to submit feedback directly to PEFA. All submissions are received,
indexed and filed alongside existing expressions of sentiment.

> “We were told iPhone users represent an exceptionally high-value demographic. We have therefore
> invited them to test our product for free, which we consider a generous concession.”
>
> — C. Marchetti-Brandt, Chief Supporter Tolerance Officer

Joining the open test requires the free TestFlight app from the App Store and the public join link.
No separate invitation from the Global Inclusion Committee is required at this stage.

The Superior League app™ is now in open iOS testing on [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/zbyB4qFD),
live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.howeitis.superiorleague), and
available in any modern browser on [the web](https://superior-league-2036.vercel.app/).

**Notes to editors**

PEFA™ is the owner and operator of the Superior League. Participating in a beta programme does not
constitute consultation, representation or a vote.
