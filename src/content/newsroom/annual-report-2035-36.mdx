PEFA today published its first Annual Report, covering the period from incorporation on 14 May
2035 to 30 June 2036.

Highlights for the period:

- Revenue: guaranteed, irrespective of results
- Variance to plan: nil
- Supporter Tolerance Index: 112, against a target of 100
- Relegations: none
- Results: unscripted

The report sets out PEFA's five stated objectives. Three are redacted in the public filing, in line
with previous practice.

> "Our first year delivered everything we expected, which is what we designed it to do."
>
> — M. Thornbury, Chief Financial Officer

The full report is available from [Investor Relations](/investors/annual-report).
